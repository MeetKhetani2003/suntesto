Website Media & Assets Checklist

Media Sizing, Layout Aspect Ratios, and Upload Guidelines

⚠️ Critical Asset Rules (Please Read Before Creating Media)

  • Transparency (PNG): Product cards/collages MUST use transparent PNG files with no grey/pink box backgrounds, so drop-shadows and color arches align correctly.
  • Crop Bounds: Crop empty transparent space tightly around products to prevent packaging from appearing smaller than intended.
  • Video Compression: Compress loop video clips (MP4 format, H.264 codec) under 10MB to maintain rapid page loads.
  • Mobile vs Desktop: Separate assets are provided for desktop and mobile banner uploads to ensure they fit correctly on tall screens.
